# Research Note NN: Literal Question

## Decision in One Sentence

State `advance`, `revise with new data`, or `stop`, with the decisive evidence.

## Why This Matters

Describe the portfolio or market decision and the economic mechanism. Avoid a
generic statement that prediction is useful.

## Hypothesis

State the expected sign, horizon, condition, alternative explanation, and
falsification condition fixed before outcomes.

## Data and Timing

List universe, dates, provider, availability convention, revision status,
dynamic eligibility, and unresolved coverage limitations.

## Method

Name the frozen benchmark, controls, costs, evaluation split, uncertainty
method, and multiplicity family. Explain only choices that affect inference.

## Results

Report the complete primary result and sensitivity, including negative and
excluded cases. Do not lead with the best specification.

## Portfolio Interpretation

Explain what a strategy researcher may and may not change because of the result.

## Limitations

Connect each limitation to the claim it weakens.

## Reproduction

Link the command, frozen config, input and output manifests, and executing
commit.

## Interview Summary

In five sentences: question, mechanism, hardest data problem, decisive result,
and next independent test.
